Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy applies specifically to the SkyBlitzzz Android aircraft game, published by Adilabz-Orgsec, including its optional Google account, cloud-save, advertising and purchase features.

Children and age groups · Data we use · Advertising · Cloud saves · Deletion · Contact

Your choices

You can play using a local profile without creating an account. Ads that award gems, a temporary aircraft pass or a revival are optional and are offered with the reward explained before you choose to watch. Ordinary gameplay does not require watching a rewarded ad or buying an aircraft.

For adult profiles, Google sign-in and cloud backup are optional. Signing in to cloud saves is separate from Google Play Games features such as leaderboards. A cloud backup must be saved before it can be restored on another device.

Children and age groups

SkyBlitzzz includes children in its audience. A neutral age-group screen appears before advertising or account features are enabled. We store only the selected age band on the device; we do not request an exact birth date. Unknown age profiles use protective defaults.

Profiles under 18 use local gameplay and cannot create a SkyBlitzzz Firebase account, upload a cloud save or sign in to Google Play Games through the game. We do not operate a parental-consent account service. Parents can contact us if they believe a child has provided account information in error.

Ads for children use child-directed settings. Teen profiles receive under-age-of-consent treatment. Advertising content is restricted to the general-audience rating. We do not request Android's advertising-ID permission, precise location, contacts, messages, microphone or camera access.

Data we use and why

DataPurpose and handling
Local game progressScores, coins, gems, owned and equipped aircraft, temporary passes, purchase entitlement status, upgrades/parts, Classic medals and best times, daily goals, reward receipts and settings let the game continue and prevent duplicate rewards. These are stored on your device. Personal ghost recordings are local replay files.
Adult Google account detailsIf you choose Google sign-in, Google/Firebase Authentication processes the authentication credential, account ID, email and basic profile details to identify the account. We use the Firebase user ID to associate your cloud save with you. The game does not receive your Google password.
Adult cloud-save snapshotIf you choose cloud backup, game progress and settings are sent to Google Cloud Firestore under your account. A saved profile includes balances, aircraft ownership/access, temporary-pass expiry, medals, scores, parts and goal/reward state. Authentication credentials and private ghost replay files are not placed in the game-progress document.
Google Play Games informationFor adult profiles using configured Play Games features, Google processes the player identity and submitted scores/achievement events. Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players according to Google's settings. Cloud-save sign-in does not automatically publish your entire game profile.
Purchase informationGoogle Play Billing handles payment details. The game uses product IDs, purchase status and purchase tokens to process and restore aircraft entitlements. We do not receive your full card number or banking password. Restoring a cloud profile does not independently prove a paid purchase.
Ad and technical informationGoogle Mobile Ads may process IP address, approximate region inferred from IP, app/ad interactions, performance diagnostics and permitted app/device identifiers to provide ads and prevent fraud. Child/teen treatment and your consent choices constrain ad processing. Android advertising-ID permission is removed in this app.
Support requestsIf you contact us, we use the information you send, such as your email and account identifier, to answer the request or verify a deletion request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or a child's identity documents.

The game does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Advertising services still have their own measurement and diagnostic processing as described above.

Advertising and consent

SkyBlitzzz uses Google AdMob for a menu banner and explicitly chosen rewarded ads. A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms it was earned. Declining, cancelling or an unavailable ad does not block ordinary gameplay. The game does not show automatic full-screen ads at startup, level start or while you are flying.

Google's consent interface is used where required. When available, Settings provides advertising privacy choices. You can review Google's privacy policy and Mobile Ads data disclosures. No third-party ad-mediation adapters are included in this version.

Storage, sharing and security

Local progress is stored in the app's private storage. It is not advertised as encrypted at rest. Cloud saves are associated with the signed-in adult's Firebase account and stored in Firestore in Mumbai, India. Access rules restrict game-save documents to their authenticated owner. Network communication with the services uses HTTPS/TLS.

We use Google/Firebase as service providers for authentication and cloud storage, and Google Play for purchases and game services. Google AdMob processes ad information. Your cloud-save document is not sent to the ad service as an advertising profile. We do not sell game-account or cloud-save information. We may disclose information where required by law or to address abuse/security issues.

Google services may process authentication, advertising, support and diagnostic information in other countries. See Firebase's privacy and security information. This static policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information when you visit it. The page has no analytics script, advertising or account login.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains until you clear the game's app data, uninstall it or replace/reset the local profile. Cloud progress remains until you delete the cloud account/save or ask us to delete it. Signing out alone does not delete the cloud save. Deleting a cloud account does not automatically delete Google Play purchase records or independently managed Play Games data.

An adult can use Settings' cloud-account deletion action to remove the SkyBlitzzz cloud-save document and Firebase account. Recent Google authentication may be required. You can also request deletion after uninstalling through our account and data deletion page. We verify ownership before acting; contact us if the in-app action cannot finish.

Service-provider security logs and purchase records may be retained according to their policies and applicable requirements. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information and withdraw consent. Use the in-app choices or contact us.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the game's data handling changes. Material changes will also be reflected in the game's notices or release information.
