Account and data deletion

Last updated: 9 October 2026. You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the game.

Inside the game

Open Settings, find Cloud saves, then choose the account deletion action. Confirm the deletion and, if requested, sign in again to verify ownership. This removes the SkyBlitzzz cloud save and Firebase authentication account.

After uninstalling, or if deletion cannot finish

Email adilabz1405@gmail.com with the subject “SkyBlitzzz account and data deletion”. Identify the Google account email used for SkyBlitzzz. We will confirm ownership and the scope of the deletion before processing it.

A parent or guardian can use the same contact for concerns about a child's information. Do not send passwords, card details or identity documents.

What is deleted

The account's Firestore game-save document and Firebase authentication record, including saved game progress and its association with the account, are deleted. Signing out is not deletion.

What is separate

Clear SkyBlitzzz's app data or uninstall it to remove local progress and ghost recordings. Google Play purchase records, Play Games leaderboard/achievement data and ad-provider logs are managed separately by Google and are subject to their controls and retention policies. Deleting the SkyBlitzzz account does not cancel or refund a Play purchase. Service-provider security or legally required records may be retained under their applicable policies.
